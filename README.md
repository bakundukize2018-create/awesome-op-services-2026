## 📱 What Is a Virtual Phone Number?

A virtual phone number is a telephone number that can be used online without requiring a traditional physical SIM card in your phone.

Depending on the number and service, you may be able to receive SMS messages, verification codes (OTP), and calls.

Virtual numbers can be useful for individuals, freelancers, entrepreneurs, developers, marketers and businesses working with online platforms.
